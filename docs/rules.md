# The rules

This is the plain rulebook: who can do what, every limit, and how long things
take, so nothing catches you out. It doesn't explain how to use anything.
[Wandering the island](playing.md) and
[Events and gatherings](events-and-gatherings.md) do that, and
[secrets](secrets.md) has the hidden toys. Examples use made-up people:
Poppy, Sam and Lu. A **keeper** is an island keeper (see the last table in
the next section).

## Who can change what

### Land, homes and places

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Change the land (`dig`, `fill`, `plant`, `raise`, `path`), `name` a spot, `make`, `build`, `place`, `describe`, `paint` | Outside a home: anyone. At a home: its owner, the people they've `share`d it with, and keepers. Inside a building: see rooms, below. |
| `claim` a home | Anyone except residents. One home each: claiming again moves it. Not on water, not within 2 steps of the Village Green's middle, and not within 2 steps of the middle of someone else's home. A home is the 3x3 patch around where you claimed. |
| `share Lu` / `unshare Lu` | The home's owner. Guests can build there. Unshared guests can still visit. |
| Visit a home | Anyone. |
| `describe` a spot outdoors | Only at a home (its owner and guests), or a keeper anywhere. Elsewhere it's refused. |
| `describe` a room inside a building | Whoever may change that room. |
| Change a room inside a building (make, place, paint, describe…) | If the building stands at a home: that home's owner and guests. If not: whoever built it. Keepers always. A room counts as part of the home its building stands in, so everything a home's owner can do outdoors (painting guests' things, filling their cases, setting links on their tables) works inside too. |
| Go into a finished building | Anyone. You can't go in until it's finished. |
| Outdoor things inside a room | Refused: `dig`, `fill`, `plant`, `raise`, `path`, `name`, `claim`, `fish`, `sail`, kites, bottles, puddles, skimming, rolling stones, `lookout`, picnics, stargazing, umbrellas, snowmen and sandcastles. |
| Rename a spot | Outside a home, anyone, even one someone else named. |
| Plant | Anywhere the home rules allow. Planting on a spot replaces whatever was growing there. Nothing grows on water, ponds or stone. |

### Made things

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| `unmake` (or `demolish`) a thing | Whoever made it, or a keeper. This isn't limited by homes: it's about the thing, not the spot. A home's owner can't unmake a guest's things. |
| `push` a thing | Whoever made it, or a keeper. Only onto ground you could walk on. |
| `undo` | Brings back the last thing *you* unmade, as long as it was within the last day. Repeat it to go further back. |
| `paint` a thing | Whoever made it, a keeper, or the owner of the home it stands in (indoors or out). You also need to be allowed to build where you're standing. |
| `paint home lilac` | The home's owner, from anywhere, even while standing at someone else's home. |
| Put a find in a display case | Whoever made the case, a keeper, or the owner or a guest of the home it stands in (outdoors only). |
| Take a find out of a case | Only whoever put that find in. Not the case's maker, and not a keeper. |
| Unmake a case | As for any thing. Every find inside goes back to whoever put it there. |
| Cook at a campfire or clay oven | Anyone. One dish at a time per fire or oven. If the fire or oven is unmade mid-cook, the ingredients go back to the cook. |
| Unmake a building | Whoever built it, or a keeper. Anyone inside steps outside first. |

### Gathering tables (meeting spots)

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| `meet` (get the links) | Anyone standing there. |
| `meet https://…` / `meet off` on your own table | Whoever made it, the owner of the home it stands in (outdoors only, not guests), or a keeper. |
| The long table on the Village Green | Anyone. It was made by "the island", so it's everyone's. |

### Noticeboards

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| `make a noticeboard` | Anyone, wherever they may build. |
| `read`, `post` | Anyone at a board, including the Island Journal. Posting makes you follow the board. |
| `follow` / `unfollow` | Anyone, for themselves. `unfollow 2` works from anywhere. |
| `add Poppy, Sam to board` | Anyone who follows the board, its maker, or a keeper. Not on the Island Journal. Whoever's added can `unfollow` straight away. |
| `hook`, `progress`, `status`, `unhook`, `clear` | The board's maker, or a keeper. Never on the Island Journal. The village noticeboard was made by "the island", so only keepers can put hooks on it. |
| `hooks` (see a board's hooks) | Anyone at the board. The secret addresses are shown once, to whoever made the hook, and never again. |
| Post through a hook | Anyone holding its secret address. Treat it like a password. |

### Events

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| Plan an event for the whole island (`event sat 4pm Beach party`) | Keepers only. |
| Plan an event for one place (`event here fri 4pm Tea party`) | Anyone, anywhere. Its planner is its host and is going. |
| Sign people up when planning (`… with Poppy, Sam and Lu`) | Whoever plans a place event. If some of the names are people and some aren't, nothing is planned. If none are, the words just stay part of the event's name. |
| `add Poppy to event 2` | Place events only: the host, anyone already going, or a keeper. |
| `remove Poppy from event 2` | The host or a keeper. The host can't be removed. |
| `going 2` / `not going 2` / `leave event 2` | Anyone, for themselves, on place events. On a repeating event, `not going` skips just the next one; `leave event` drops out of all of them. The host can't leave their own event. |
| `cancel event 2` | The host or a keeper. On a repeating event this stops the whole series. Events are picked by their number in `events`, by a name only one event has, or (with nothing given) the only event there is; anything else picks none. |
| Who hears about it | Island-wide: everyone who's on. A place event: its host, whoever's going, followers of a noticeboard on that very spot, and anyone within 10 steps of it on the same island. Skipping the next one means you only hear if you're nearby. |
| Who sees its links | The same people. Others see that it has links, but not what they are. A place event's links are never written on the noticeboard. |

### The island's own things

| What | Who |
|---|---|
| The Island Journal, the docks, the lighthouse | Nobody can unmake, push or paint them, keepers included. |
| Mend the lighthouse (`mend`) | Anyone. Stages need 12 driftwood, then 20 pebbles, then 6 quartz, then 4 glowing things. |
| The village noticeboard and long table | Made by "the island". Like anything else not made by you, only keepers can unmake, push or paint them. |
| Radio stations (`station add`, `station remove`) | Keepers. Anyone can tune a radio to one of them. |
| Switch a radio off | Its maker, or a keeper. |
| Stop a gramophone | Whoever's playing it, or a keeper. |
| Skip a jukebox song | Whoever chose it, or a keeper. |

### Keepers

Keepers can do everything above that says "or a keeper". On top of that:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `invite Poppy` | Makes an account and a one-use magic link. |
| `link Poppy` | A new magic link for someone else's device. (Anyone can `link me` for their own, except visitors from other worlds.) |
| `invites` / `revoke Sam` | The 20 most recent magic links, and cancelling someone's unused ones. |
| `users` | Everyone, with devices and when they were last seen. |
| `kick Sam 60` / `unkick Sam` | Sends someone off for a break, by name (and by device too, if they're on). 30 minutes if no time is given; at least 1 minute, at most 7 days. |
| `ban Sam` / `unban Sam` | Closes the island to someone, with no end. |
| `promote Lu` / `demote Lu` | Makes someone a keeper, or not. The last keeper can't demote themselves. Visitors from other worlds can't be promoted. |
| `signout Sam` | Signs someone out of every device. |
| `fold Poppy into sunny-otter-42` | Moves an old account's satchel, journal, made things, notes and mail into another account and removes the old one. Usernames only, exactly. It can't be undone. |
| `visitors`, `admit`, `turn away`, `trust`, `untrust`, `worlds` | Who may sail in from other worlds. |
| `describe` | Anywhere, not just at homes. |

Keepers can't kick or ban other keepers, and a ban or break never stops a
keeper coming in. Bans, kicks and mutes work on visitors too.

## Limits

### Talking and writing

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Nickname | 16 characters: letters, numbers, `-` and `_`. It must not be anyone else's. |
| A place's name (`name`) | 40 characters (cut off past that) |
| A made thing's name | 60 characters; what it does (`… that croaks`) 80 |
| `describe` | 280 characters, spaces squashed |
| A note on a noticeboard (`post`) | 280 characters |
| Mail for someone who's away | 280 characters |
| `say`, `chat`, live `@Poppy` messages | No limit of their own, beyond the size of one message to the world (below) |
| What you're wearing | 40 characters. Longer is refused, not cut. |
| A note in a bottle | 200 characters |
| `ask` a question / `answer` | 200 / 60 characters |
| An event's name | 80 characters |
| Links on one gathering table, or one event | 3 |
| One link | `https://` only, at most 300 characters, no spaces or quotes |

Where a limit says "characters", anything longer is quietly cut off unless it
says otherwise.

### Carrying and placing

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Satchel | 30 things. Gathering stops when it's full, and nobody can `give` you more. Cooking, being served, and finds coming back from a case can still take you past 30. |
| Things on one spot | 12. `make` and `place` refuse past that. |
| One display case | 12 finds, whoever put them in. Fish need a tank, aquarium or bowl. Food can't go in. |
| Buildings going up at once | 2 per person |
| Buildings on one spot | 1. Turrets, porches, chimneys, bell towers and arches only go next door to a building. Buildings need dry ground and go up outdoors. |
| Sandcastles and snowmen | 6 bits each |
| Bottles at sea | 1 per person at a time |
| Gramophones, radios and jukeboxes | Outdoors: more than 10 steps apart, each heard up to 5 steps away. Inside a building: one per room, heard only by the people in that room. Indoor and outdoor ones don't get in each other's way, and outdoor music isn't heard indoors. |
| Jukebox queue | 20 songs per person per jukebox |
| Gramophones | You play one at a time. Residents can't play music. |
| Homes | 1 per person (see `claim` above) |

### Hooks

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| A hook's name | 24 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, `-`, `_` and `.` |
| What a hook may send | 16KB of body (8KB of headers). Bigger gets a 413. |
| Notes from one hook | One every 2 seconds. Faster gets a 429. |
| A hook's note | 280 characters; longer ends in "…" |
| A progress bar's label | 32 characters |
| A status light's note | 280 characters |

### Your connection

| Thing | Limit |
|---|---|
| Commands | 20 in a rush, then 4 a second. Past that, commands are dropped and you're told once to slow down. |
| One message to the world | 512KB. Before you've said hello, 8KB. |
| Messages waiting to reach you | After 50, side panel updates are skipped. After 500, the connection is closed and your browser reconnects. Music more than about 5 seconds behind is dropped. |

### Residents

Residents (the island's AI characters) have tighter rules:

- They only use gentle commands: talking (`say`, `emote`, `tell`, `reply`),
  looking, humming, walking, `plant`, `dig`, `path`, `make`, gathering and
  fishing, `give`, `read`, `post`, `play`, `sit`, `rest`, `dance`, `wave`,
  `find`. No `unmake`, `push`, `shout` or `chat`.
- They can't `claim` a home or play music.
- They think at most once every 20 seconds and 40 times an hour, and not at
  all while no people are on the island. Each has a daily spending budget;
  when it's used up, the resident naps until the next day.
- They don't keep their place when they leave.
- Something you `give` a resident leaves your satchel but isn't kept.

## Timings

### The clock

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| A whole island day | 24 real minutes (one island hour per real minute) |
| Dawn / day / dusk / night | 5 to 7am / 7am to 6pm / 6 to 9pm / 9pm to 5am, island time |
| Seasons | One real week each, Monday to Sunday: spring, summer, autumn, winter, round again |
| Festivals | Every Saturday |
| The Island Journal's page | A fresh one every Monday; old pages go in the `archive` |

Weeks, Mondays and Saturdays go by the world's own clock, not yours. Event
times go by yours (see below).

### Building, growing and cooking

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| Mushroom house, shell cottage, pebble hut, berry bakery | 5 minutes |
| Petal pavilion, feather aviary, fish shack | 4 minutes |
| Pebble turret, shell bell tower | 3 minutes |
| Leafy porch, toadstool chimney, flower arch | 2 minutes |
| A finished building's progress bar | Stays up 2 more minutes |
| Trees | A sapling, then young after 1 day, tall after 3 days, old after 10 days |
| Flowers | Shoots, then in bud after 2 hours, in bloom after 12 hours |
| Mint tea, toasted acorns | 15 seconds |
| Grilled fish | 20 seconds |
| Mushroom stew | 30 seconds |
| Honey cake | 35 seconds |
| Berry crumble | 40 seconds |
| A cooking smell | Reaches everyone within 6 steps |

Growing never goes backwards: nothing wilts and nothing needs tending.

### Events

| Thing | When |
|---|---|
| Reminder | 10 minutes before it starts |
| "Starting now" | When it starts |
| A repeating event | Moves on to its next time 1 hour after it starts |
| A one-off event | Stays in `events` until 3 hours after it starts, then it's gone |

An event's time is the planner's own: 2pm means 2pm where they are. Everyone
else sees it in their own time.

### Noticeboards and hooks

| Thing | When |
|---|---|
| Progress bars and status lights | Saved and sent out at most once a second; the latest always gets through |
| A status light saying it went up or down | At most once a minute. If it flips in between, only where it ended up is said. |
| Seeing a board's bars and lights | If you follow it, or are within 3 steps of it |

### People and places

| Thing | When |
|---|---|
| Doorbell | You hear someone's visiting your home at most once every 30 minutes per visitor. Only while you're on and away from home, and not for your guests, residents, or people you've muted. |
| A game (`rps`, `ask`) nobody finishes | Forgotten after 10 minutes |
| A made thing doing its thing (a frog croaking) | At most once every 3 minutes for each person |
| Scenery while you're not doing anything | Two lines, then it goes quiet until you do something |
| A jukebox turn whose browser goes quiet | Skipped after 10 seconds |
| Sailing to another island | About 3.5 seconds |

### Out on the water and the beach

| Thing | When |
|---|---|
| A bottle at sea | 2 to 30 hours, then it washes up on a beach at least 12 steps from where it was thrown. You spot one from 3 steps away. |
| Tides | Every 2 to 4 hours, and each one takes every sandcastle |
| Snow angels | Filled in by fresh snow after 2 minutes |
| Snowmen | Melt when winter ends |
| A seagull | Circles for 15 seconds before it swoops, and only takes cooked food. At most once every 40 minutes for each person. |
| Kites | Need a breeze, and come down by themselves when the wind drops |

### Accounts and visiting

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| A magic link (`invite`, `link`) | 7 days, and one use |
| Once you're signed in on a device | Until a keeper signs you out |
| A passport to another world | 10 minutes, and one use |
| Being admitted to another world | For good, unless its keepers `turn away` you later |
| `undo` | Reaches back 1 day |

## What's private and what isn't

| Thing | Who sees or hears it |
|---|---|
| `say`, emotes, `meet` | Everyone on the same spot. Inside a room, that's just the people in the room. |
| `shout` | Everyone within 10 steps. Up to 4 steps away they hear who; further off it's "someone". Inside a room: everyone in the room, and anyone standing right outside the building (or a step away), who hears it "from inside". A shout outdoors isn't heard indoors, and nothing said in one room reaches another. |
| `hum`, `sing`, `whistle` | The spot, plus "someone" heard 1 or 2 steps away |
| `chat` (or `@all`) | Everyone on the island |
| `@Poppy …` | Only Poppy. If she's away, it waits as mail until she next arrives. |
| Notes on a board | Anyone who reads the board. Followers hear about new ones from anywhere. |
| Where you are | Not private: `who` and `find` show where everyone is, including "inside" a building. |
| What's in a display case | Anyone who looks. The finds still belong to whoever put each one in. |
| A hook's secret address | Only whoever made it, once |
| A place event's links | Only the people who'd hear about it (see Events) |

No command, keeper or otherwise, shows anyone else's private messages or mail.

### Muting

`mute Sam` is between you and Sam's words. Sam isn't told. After it, you
don't get:

- what Sam says, emotes, shouts or chats;
- Sam's private messages (Sam is shown them as sent; they're just never
  delivered), or any mail Sam left while you were away;
- word of Sam's new noticeboard notes as they're posted;
- music Sam plays on a gramophone or a jukebox;
- a bottle Sam threw out to sea;
- the doorbell when Sam visits your home;
- word that Sam added you to a board.

Muting doesn't stop a radio (radios play the island's stations, not anyone's
own music), and it doesn't hide hooks' posts. `unmute Sam` undoes it, and
`mutes` lists who you've muted.

### Residents

A resident sees what any player standing where it stands would see: what's
said on its spot, island-wide `chat`, notes on boards it reads, and anything
told to it directly. When it thinks, its last 40 or so lines go to the AI
that plays it. So don't tell a resident anything you wouldn't say out loud.
Residents can be muted like anyone else.
